# Thrifted Tease V2 Homepage Brief

## Purpose
This document defines what the homepage should do in V2, what should change from the current version, and what content it needs to carry.

## Core Job
The homepage should feel like the band's front door, not just the site's directory page.

Its job is to:
- establish mood immediately
- make the band feel memorable
- route visitors into listening, shows, merch, and media

## Current Homepage Problems
- The hero positioning is safe and generic.
- The current subtitle frames the site as an official destination instead of a compelling band world.
- The streaming section is useful, but its language is generic.
- The photo strip functions more like an intro gallery than a brand-building editorial block.
- The current page is clear, but not yet magnetic.

## What Must Be True In V2
- A visitor should get a strong impression before reading much.
- The first CTA hierarchy should be unmistakable.
- The homepage should balance vibe and utility.
- The page should borrow authenticity from the media archive instead of trying to fake it through polish.

## Recommended Content Blocks

### 1. Hero
Needs:
- a sharper headline or positioning line
- one supporting line with real voice
- primary CTA
- secondary CTA

Possible CTA patterns:
- Listen
- See Shows
- View Archive
- Shop The Drop

### 2. Featured Music
Needs:
- latest release, featured track, or primary listening destination
- one short context line

This should feel like discovery, not platform administration.

### 3. Band Statement
Needs:
- a short paragraph or a few lines that express the band in its own voice

This block should answer:
Why does this band feel like this band?

### 4. Live Signal
Needs:
- one upcoming show or a compact live module
- enough proof that the band is active in real rooms

### 5. Merch Signal
Needs:
- one featured item, collection, or current drop
- a stronger reason to care than "merch exists"

### 6. Curated Media Strip
Needs:
- a tighter, more cinematic pull from the existing archive
- fewer but stronger images than a general gallery introduction

### 7. Footer And Contact
Needs:
- direct booking/contact path
- core social links
- concise supporting information

## Design Guidance
- Use stronger editorial pacing than V1.
- Avoid presenting every block as the same card type.
- Let the hero and media strip do most of the emotional lifting.
- Keep the page scannable on mobile.

## Copy Guidance
- Avoid "official band site" energy.
- Avoid overly broad genre labeling as the lead message.
- Avoid platform-first language.
- Use specific, concise lines that carry atmosphere.

## Homepage Success Criteria
- Visitors understand the mood quickly.
- Primary actions are obvious without being shouted.
- The page feels like Thrifted Tease before it feels like a website.
- The page makes the rest of the site more interesting to click into.
