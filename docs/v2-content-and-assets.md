# Thrifted Tease V2 Content And Assets Plan

## Purpose
This document defines the content and asset priorities needed to make V2 feel specific, current, and believable.

## Main Principle
V2 should get stronger by curating better, not only by adding more.

## Strongest Existing Asset Classes
- venue photography
- band portraits
- behind-the-scenes recording photos
- optimized homepage and media derivatives
- hero imagery already prepared for key pages

## Highest Priority Content Gaps

### 1. Homepage copy
Needs:
- stronger hero positioning
- band statement in real voice
- clearer CTA hierarchy

### 2. Merch storytelling
Needs:
- product framing tied to the band's identity
- real imagery or more convincing final presentation
- more specific product copy

### 3. Shows credibility content
Needs:
- real upcoming dates where possible
- a fallback system for periods with few or no active dates
- supporting venue proof

### 4. Media curation
Needs:
- ranking of strongest venue folders
- selection of the best lead images
- tighter captions

## Asset Priorities For V2

### Must-have
- final homepage hero image direction
- best-of archive selects for homepage and media
- real merch visuals or stronger final mockups
- selected venue proof for shows

### Nice-to-have
- flyer textures, posters, or ephemera details
- a dedicated set of portrait crops for band storytelling modules
- a more deliberate set of page hero images

## Curation Rules
- lead with the strongest images, not the most complete folders
- avoid using weak images just to fill symmetry
- choose assets that reinforce the band's mood and room presence
- treat repetitive near-duplicates as editing problems, not layout opportunities

## Content Production Recommendations

### Copy
Create:
- homepage hero options
- short band bio options
- page intro options for media, shows, and merch
- merch item descriptions in band voice

### Photography
Organize:
- best-of live selects
- best-of portraits
- best BTS and rehearsal details

### Merch
Prepare:
- real product photos or more final product renders
- item names and any real drop framing
- optional sizing, release, or stock notes if true

## Metadata And Supporting Content
V2 should also improve:
- page titles
- meta descriptions
- social sharing image strategy
- alt text quality

## Content Review Checklist
- Is this asset one of the strongest available?
- Does this copy sound like the band?
- Is the content helping prove the world, or just filling space?
- Would removing this piece make the page stronger?
