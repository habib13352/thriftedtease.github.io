# Thrifted Tease — Website Launch Checklist

**Goal:** Launch a website that looks like us, has accurate information, and lets people listen, contact us, and buy shirts through Square.

**Pace:** 1–3 hours per week. Estimated work: 18–26 hours, including testing and buffer. At 2 hours per week, allow roughly 9–13 weeks, plus any delays collecting assets.

**Shared folder:** __________ (Brand / Merch / Media)  
**Agreed input deadline:** __________  
**Launch target:** __________ (confirm once inputs and checkout are ready)

## 1. Band inputs

- [ ] **Spencer:** Share current album art, logos, Canva links, fonts, and graphics. Include originals/high-resolution exports and mark what is current.
- [ ] **Kevin, Bennett, Hamza, Spencer:** Each send 2–3 colour ideas, 1–2 visual references, and colours to avoid. Aim for vibrant, vintage, Patagonia-inspired colour with readable text. Include your opinion on gold.
- [ ] **Everyone:** Nominate favourite photos and direct YouTube video links, including new material. Add the show/session, date if known, caption, and credit. Flag old or repetitive material to remove from the website.
- [ ] **Everyone:** Confirm upcoming shows and ticket links, or confirm there are no announced dates.

## 2. Hamza — build and prepare

- [ ] Confirm the launch shirts: names, prices/currency, sizes, stock, sizing details, and shipping/pickup.
- [ ] Take real shirt photos with consistent lighting and plain backgrounds. Replace mockups and Shopify links with real products and matching Square checkout links.
- [ ] Verify each product's sizes, price, stock, and fulfillment. Complete a purchase and refund; check the receipt, order notification, taxes, and inventory update. **Working sales are required for launch.**
- [ ] Prepare two colour previews; collect one band response and apply the agreed palette consistently.
- [ ] Publish **8–12 selected photos and two verified YouTube videos**, accurately labelled and captioned. Remove empty sections and weak duplicates; keep original files archived.
- [ ] Remove outdated upcoming shows and placeholder copy. Check music/social links and confirm the contact/booking inbox receives email.

## 3. Ready to go live

- [ ] **Hamza:** Check all four pages on phone and desktop: readable text, clear images, working menus, links, videos, keyboard controls, and no broken assets.
- [ ] **Band:** Review one complete preview and send one consolidated list of fixes. Separate launch problems from ideas for later.
- [ ] **Hamza:** Resolve checkout, accuracy, and usability problems; record the launch decision.
- [ ] **Hamza:** Publish, then recheck all four pages and every purchase link at the public URL. Save the previous working version for rollback.

## 4. After launch

- [ ] **Hamza:** Reserve 15–20 minutes weekly for site checks. Keep show listings current and check orders/stock as sales occur.
- [ ] Improve one thing at a time: shirt hover/detail views, more band artwork, a featured release, fresh media, or sharing previews.

**Weekly rule:** Finish one 30–60 minute task before starting another. Keep updates simple:
